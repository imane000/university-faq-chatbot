# Demonstration corpus

These files were already included in the repository. Their text identifies them as
Sorbonne Université publications; they are retained as an academic demonstration
corpus, with ownership remaining with their authors/publishers. This project is not
an official university service. Original download URLs and redistribution terms
were not recorded in the initial repository and have not been independently verified.

Review the year and faculty before interpreting an answer. Dates and fees from this
corpus must not be presented as current advice for 2026–2027. The older guide includes
published association contact addresses and named institutional representatives.
No private student record or filled personal dossier was identified in the extracted
text review. PDF images and every possible hidden identifier are not covered by
that review. Before extending or redistributing the corpus, verify source URLs,
permissions and personal-data content. Use only documents you are authorized to share.

Ingestion reads only `faq.json` and top-level `*.pdf` files; this inventory is not indexed.
